Welcome to Hanzi Project(仮)!

The purpose of this program is to estimate how many Chinese characters you can recognize.

Questions adjust to your ability!

Start Assessment

    How it works:
  1. First, choose whether you want to be tested on recognition of common Chinese characters, or try challenge mode and see if you can recognize rarer characters.
  2. Next, choose the script you want to be tested on. For the beta version, traditional is the only script available. I will soon expand to include simplified, Japanese, and Cantonese character testing.
  3. Choose your preferred input method. Zhuyin (bopomofo) uses the Taiwanese typing method, while pinyin uses the Latin alphabet, and kana uses Japanese hiragana and katakana. Kana will be added when the Japanese version is complete.
  4. Finally, choose whether you want to take your test to the next level and include the tone mark in your answer. For example, hao3 for pinyin or ㄏㄠˇ for zhuyin.
  5. Start the assessment, and see as the adaptive difficulty will gradually show you less common characters, asking you to prove you know it by entering the correct pronunciation using one of the input methods mentioned earlier, and the more you play, the more accurate your estimated character recognition number will be!
